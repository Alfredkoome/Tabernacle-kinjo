About Our Church

We are an international church where people can meet with God, be transformed by His love expressed through the Gospel and each other, and be sent out as His instruments to bring God's transformation to this world.
Meeting Kinjo area of Meru since 1980,GTC has been striving to the international community in Meru,by providing globally relevant christian fellowship and teaching.
The strength of GTC's ministry rest in Christ-centered worship,Biblically-sound preaching, quality children's ministry ,and life-changing friendships in discipleship groups. We are delighted to welcome you to our church family.This is a place
of love ,hope,and spiritual grouth where every is accepted and valued.Whether you are visiting for the first time or have been with us for years,we are happy to have you here.Join us we worship,learn God's word,and grow together in faith and unity.
